About YOYO

Bridging the gap between customers and trusted service providers.

Who we are

YOYO is a home services platform built to solve a problem every household eventually runs into: finding someone reliable to fix things. Whether it's a leaking pipe at midnight, a flickering light switch, or a washing machine that's stopped spinning, most people don't have a trusted technician's number saved in their phone. YOYO exists to close that gap.

We connect homeowners and businesses with background-checked, skilled professionals across plumbing, electrical work, carpentry, appliance repair, cleaning, and more — all bookable through a simple platform with transparent, upfront pricing.

Our story

YOYO started with a simple, frustrating experience. Late one evening, a burst pipe left a small apartment flooding while its owner scrambled through old business cards and half-remembered phone numbers, trying to find anyone who could come fix it. After an hour of unanswered calls and one technician who quoted triple the fair price once he saw the panic in her voice, the job finally got done — but the experience left a lasting impression: finding reliable help at home shouldn't be this hard, or this risky.

That frustration became the seed of an idea. What if booking a trusted technician could be as simple and predictable as ordering food online — a few taps, a clear price, and someone qualified showing up on time? YOYO was built to answer that question. We started small, verifying a handful of electricians and plumbers in Kathmandu, and grew city by city as more households discovered they no longer had to gamble on who showed up at their door.

Today, YOYO connects hundreds of households with verified professionals every month, but the mission hasn't changed since that first flooded apartment: make trustworthy help easy to find, and easy to trust.

How it works

Getting help through YOYO starts with browsing our list of service categories — plumbing, electrical, carpentry, appliance repair, cleaning, and more. Each listed professional shows their rating, years of experience, and a clear price estimate before you ever commit to booking, so there are no surprises once the job begins.

Once you select a technician and confirm your booking, YOYO sends a confirmation with the professional's details and an estimated arrival window. On the day of service, you'll get a live notification when they're on their way, so you're never left wondering if help is actually coming.

After the work is completed, payment is only processed once you've confirmed you're satisfied with the job — and every booking is backed by a 30-day service warranty. If something isn't right after the technician leaves, we send someone back to make it right, at no extra cost to you.

Our mission

We believe trust shouldn't be a luxury reserved for those with the right connections or the time to ask around. Every household — whether in a busy city apartment or a quiet suburban home — deserves access to reliable, fairly-priced help without the guesswork, the waiting, or the risk of being overcharged by someone they've never met before.

Our mission is to close that gap permanently. Not just by connecting customers with technicians, but by building a standard of trust that both sides can rely on: verified professionals who take pride in their work, and customers who know exactly what they're paying for before a single tool is picked up.

As YOYO grows beyond Kathmandu, Pokhara, and Butwal into more cities across Nepal, our goal remains the same as it was on day one — to become the first place every household turns to when something at home needs fixing, and the platform every skilled professional is proud to be listed on.

Our values

Trust first. Every relationship we build — with customers and service providers alike — is grounded in honesty and accountability. We don't list a technician we wouldn't personally hire for our own home.

Fair pay, fair prices. We believe skilled labor deserves fair compensation, and customers deserve fair prices. Our platform is built to serve both sides of that equation, not squeeze one to benefit the other.

Always improving. Every review, every completed job, and every piece of feedback shapes how we train our partner professionals and refine our platform. We're never finished improving.
